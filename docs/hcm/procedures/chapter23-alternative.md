# HCM Chapter 23, Part C — Alternative Intersections (RCUT / MUT / DLT)

This document walks through the Rust translation of HCM 7th Edition Chapter 23, Part C ("Alternative Intersection Evaluation"), which follows the same ten-step Exhibit 23-47 framework used for interchanges in Part B (`chapter23.md`), applied to restricted crossing U-turns (RCUT), median U-turns (MUT), and displaced left-turn intersections (DLT). Only the steps genuinely specific to Part C are implemented in `src/hcm/chapter23/alternative_intersections.rs`: junction-level control delays for signalized junctions and the Chapter 18 flow-profile procedure are explicitly out of scope and enter the module as pre-computed `JunctionStep::Provided` inputs, per the module doc comment's step-by-step scoping list (Steps 1-4 largely reuse Chapter 19/20/34 machinery documented elsewhere; only Steps 1's O-D redistribution, 5's U-turn crossover adjustment and DLT offset, 6's STOP-junction evaluation, 7's EDTT, 9's ETT assembly/aggregation, and 10's LOS are modeled here). Sources are EPUB `178_Ch23_pt3_01.xhtml` through `182_Ch23_pt3_05.xhtml` (introduction through applications), cross-checked against Chapter 34 Example Problems 12-17 (`269_Ch34_02b.xhtml`/`269_Ch34_02c.xhtml`, Exhibits 34-123 through 34-150). `docs/hcm/VERIFICATION.md` exists at this branch's tip with a "Chapter 23 Part C (feat/hcm-ch23-alternative-intersections)" section that is the authoritative source for the deviations below.

### Journey construction (`AltMovement`, `JunctionStep`, `AlternativeIntersection`)

`AltIntersectionForm` enumerates the six Part C forms (`RcutFourLeg`, `RcutThreeLeg`, `MutFourLeg`, `MutThreeLeg`, `DltPartial`, `DltFull`; Exhibits 23-41 through 23-45, 23-53, 23-54). An `AltMovement` is one O-D movement's journey: a label, an `Approach` (Eb/Wb/Nb/Sb, for Equation 23-61 aggregation), a demand flow rate (the aggregation weight), an ordered `Vec<JunctionStep>` (the Exhibit 23-48/23-49/23-50 traversal table for that movement, constructed by the analyst/fixture rather than derived automatically from an O-D table — the module doc comment is explicit that "the redistribution table is the analyst's input"), and a Step 7 EDTT scalar (zero for movements that are not rerouted). `JunctionStep` is a three-variant enum: `Provided` (an externally computed control delay — a Chapter 19 signalized junction, or a merge whose weaving delay is nonzero — plus `vc_gt_1`/`rq_gt_1` flags), `Stop` (evaluated in-module via `stop_junction_delay`, with optional `storage_ft`/`queue_spacing_ft` for the queue-storage-ratio LOS-F check), and `Merge` (zero delay, a free-flow merge onto the major street). `AltMovement::evaluate` walks the journey, sums the per-junction control delays, adds EDTT (Equation 23-60, `ett_s = total_control_delay_s + edtt_s`), ORs the `vc_gt_1`/`rq_gt_1` flags across every junction on the journey (forcing a stricter LOS if any junction is over capacity or over storage), and looks up LOS via `los_alternative_intersection_od`. `AlternativeIntersection` (an ordered `Vec<AltMovement>` plus the form) exposes `evaluate()` (all movements), `approach_ett(Approach)` (Equation 23-61, demand-weighted mean ETT within one approach, `None` if that approach carries no demand), and `intersection_ett()` (Equation 23-62, the same weighting over every movement).

### DLT (displaced left-turn) intersections

DLTs are modeled as an extension of the urban-street/signalized-intersection procedures rather than through the `AlternativeIntersection`/`AltMovement` journey machinery (per the module doc comment: "analyzed as extensions of the urban-street/signalized-intersection procedures"). `dlt_offset` implements the full Step 5 offset chain: Equation 23-63 (`TT_DLT = TD_DLT/(1.467 S_f,DLT)`, the displaced-left-turn roadway travel time), Equations 23-64/23-65 (`ST_DLT = LAG_DLT + O_SUPP`, `ST_TH = LAG_TH + O_MAIN`, the system start times of the DLT phase and the major-street through phase), and Equations 23-66 through 23-68 (`O_SUPP(s) = O_SUPP - ST_DLT + ST_TH - TT_DLT`, wrapped into `[0, C)` by repeated addition/subtraction of the cycle length rather than a modulo operation — functionally equivalent but written as an explicit `while` loop in the code). `DisplacedLeftTurn` holds the Exhibit 34-145/34-150 weighted-average control-delay table as `Vec<DltDelayCell>` (`flow_veh_h`, `control_delay_s` per component junction) plus the O-D demand total; `dlt_weighted_average_delay`/`DisplacedLeftTurn::intersection_ett` implement Equation 23-69 (`ETT_DLT = Sigma(d_j v_j) / Sigma v_OD`), with the doc comment noting "for DLT intersections, ETT is assumed equal to control delay (Step 7 EDTT is negligible)."

### Junction analysis reuse

Step 6's STOP-controlled junction evaluation (`stop_junction_delay`) is a thin wrapper composing three primitives from other chapters rather than new machinery: `common::gap_acceptance::potential_capacity` (Equation 20-18), `common::delay::control_delay_unsignalized` (Equation 20-61), and `chapter20::twsc::Twsc::queue_95` (Equation 20-66). Its doc comment is explicit about scope: it "models a rank-2 movement with no capacity impedance from higher-rank conflicting streams (the movement capacity equals the potential capacity), which is the situation at RCUT/MUT crossovers and the redistributed main-junction minor movements" — higher-rank impedance, when it matters, must be applied through the full Chapter 20 `Twsc` engine directly rather than through this Part-C-specific wrapper. Signalized junction delays are never recomputed by this module; they are supplied as `JunctionStep::Provided` values, which the module doc comment attributes to the Chapter 19 incremental-queue-accumulation (IQA) procedure documented in `chapter19.md`/`chapter19-actuated.md`.

## Deviations (cross-referenced to `docs/hcm/VERIFICATION.md`)

`docs/hcm/VERIFICATION.md`'s "Chapter 23 Part C (feat/hcm-ch23-alternative-intersections)" section records six items, all present at this branch's tip: (1) **DLT LOS basis** — Part C Step 10 for RCUT/MUT reads LOS from Exhibit 23-13 (`los_alternative_intersection_od`), but the DLT worked examples (Chapter 34 Examples 16/17, discussion of Exhibit 34-145) read LOS from the Chapter 19 control-delay thresholds instead (since ETT is approximately control delay for a DLT); `DisplacedLeftTurn::los` follows the worked examples (calling `common::los_tables::los_signalized_intersection`), flagged `// VERIFY-HCM` in code, with `los_alternative_intersection_od` remaining available for an analyst who prefers the Exhibit 23-13 basis. (2) **Example 15 EDTT is misprinted in the manual** — the inline equation prints `EDTT = (800+800)/(1.47*50) = 21.8 s`, apparently copy-pasted from Example 14, while the Example 15 facts state 600 ft/40 mi/h and the Exhibit 34-138 ETT column uses the correct `(600+600)/(1.47*40) = 20.4 s`; the fixture and tests use 20.4 s. (3) **Example 13 EB L ETT** — the fully computed value 55.1 s (22.9 + 16.3 + 15.9, matching the three junction-delay/EDTT components documented above) differs from the published 55.2 s by 0.1 s, attributed to intermediate rounding; LOS E either way, asserted computed within +-1 s. (4) **Equations 23-66/23-68 print errors** — Equation 23-66 prints its last term as `TT_LTD` (a typo for `TT_DLT`) and Equation 23-68 prints its guard as `if O_SUPP < C` where the accompanying prose says "lower than zero"; both are implemented per the prose/derivation rather than the literal typo (Example 16 reproduces `O_SUPP` = 45.2 s vs. the published 45 s, which rounds `TT_DLT` 6.8 to 7 s before subtracting). (5) **Example 12 inputs not fully tabulated** — the Exhibit 34-123 turning-movement demands are not present in the extracted source text, and the two major-street-left control delays (11.2/15.0 s) come from a Chapter 20 run whose conflicting flows the example does not print; both are supplied as fixture inputs, and the asserted EDTT/ETT/LOS values are independent of the unlisted demands. (6) **Scope: signalized RCUT/MUT junction delays are inputs, not recomputed** — wiring the full Chapter 19 IQA plus Chapter 18 flow-profile pipeline per signalized sub-junction (the Example 14/15 main junctions and signal crossovers) is deferred; those junction control delays enter as `Provided` steps using the Exhibit 34-132/34-137 published values, while the STOP junctions (Example 13, and the RCUT/MUT U-turn crossovers generally) are computed from first principles via `stop_junction_delay`.

## Validation

Fixtures live under `tests/ExampleCases/hcm/AlternativeIntersections/` as `case1.json` through `case4.json`, exercised by `tests/chapter23_alternative_integration.rs`. `case1.json` (Example Problem 13, three-legged RCUT with STOP signs) has every junction control delay computed in-module via `stop_junction_delay`, reproducing Exhibit 34-128 exactly: `example_13_three_legged_rcut_stop_signs` asserts six movement ETTs and LOS letters (EB L 55.2 s LOS E, EB R 22.9 s LOS C, NB L 13.0 s LOS B, NB/SB T and SB R all 0.0 s LOS A) and additionally checks the EB L journey's two individual junction delays (22.9 s main-junction right, 16.3 s U-turn crossover) and its EDTT (15.9 s) directly. `case2.json` (Example Problem 12, four-legged RCUT with merges) is EDTT-driven, with major-street left delays supplied from an external Chapter 20 run: `example_12_four_legged_rcut_merges` asserts nine movement ETT/LOS pairs against Exhibit 34-133, including the EDTT-only movements (NB/SB L and T at 55.4/60.4 s, LOS E, matching the `edtt_equations_reproduce_example_12` unit test below). `case3.json` (Example Problem 15, four-legged MUT with STOP signs at the U-turn crossovers) exercises the full Part C ETT assembly over a mix of Chapter 19/20 junction delays: `example_15_four_legged_mut_stop_signs` asserts twelve movement ETT/LOS pairs against Exhibit 34-138. `case4.json` (Example Problem 16, partial DLT) is exercised by `example_16_partial_dlt_offset_and_weighted_delay`, which checks the Step 5 offset chain directly (`dlt_offset(350.0, 35.0, 0.0, 52.0, 0.0, 0.0, 65.0)` against `tt_dlt_s` = 6.8 s at +-0.05, `st_th_s` = 52.0 s exactly, `offset_supp_s` = 45.2 s at +-0.1 against the published rounded 45 s) and then the Step 9 weighted-average delay by deserializing the fixture's `dlt` JSON block into a `DisplacedLeftTurn` and asserting `intersection_ett()` = 28.5 s/veh at +-0.1 and `los()` = LevelOfService::C. The module's documented tolerance policy (stated in the integration-test module doc comment) is O-D ETT within +-1.0 s/veh of the published Exhibit 34-129/34-133/34-138/34-145 values and O-D LOS exact, with the one sub-0.1 s delta (Example 13 EB L) called out as within tolerance and not LOS-affecting.

Unit tests in `alternative_intersections.rs`'s own `#[cfg(test)] mod unit_tests` (7 tests) spot-check the equations directly against the Chapter 34 example problems: `edtt_equations_reproduce_example_12` (Equation 23-58 against Example 12's minor-left/minor-through EDTT, 55.4/60.4 s at +-0.1); `edtt_stop_reproduces_example_13` (Equation 23-59 against Example 13's 15.9 s at +-0.1); `stop_junction_reproduces_example_13_uturn_crossover` and `stop_junction_reproduces_example_13_main_junction` (Equations 20-18/20-61/20-66 against the Exhibit 34-128 U-turn-crossover and main-junction cells, capacity/v-c/delay/Q95 all checked); `uturn_saturation_adjustment_exhibit_23_52` (the three median-width bands); `dlt_offset_reproduces_example_16` and `dlt_weighted_average_reproduces_example_16` (the same Example 16 values checked at the unit level, independent of the fixture JSON). No PyO3-binding integration test exists for Part C as of this branch (`tests/test_chapter23_integration.py` covers only Part B/interchanges, documented in `chapter23.md`).

## Deferred

Per the module doc comment's step-by-step scoping: Steps 2-3 (lane/movement groups and lane utilization at signalized junctions) are performed entirely by the Chapter 19 engine and not repeated here; Step 4 (signal progression, arrival types of Exhibit 23-51 or the Chapter 18 flow-profile procedure) is an input to the signalized-junction delay rather than computed in this module; Step 8 (additional weaving delay for an RCUT with merges) has no dedicated equation implementation — it is folded into a `Provided`/`Merge` junction step's delay by the analyst. The full Chapter 19 IQA plus Chapter 18 flow-profile pipeline per signalized sub-junction of an RCUT/MUT (rather than accepting its control delay as a `Provided` input) is deferred, per Deviation item 6 above. There is no Python-binding integration test for Part C.
